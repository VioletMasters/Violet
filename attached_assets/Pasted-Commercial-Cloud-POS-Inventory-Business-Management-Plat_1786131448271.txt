Commercial Cloud POS, Inventory & Business Management Platform

You are an elite team of senior software architects, full-stack engineers, UI/UX designers, database engineers, DevOps engineers, cybersecurity specialists, QA engineers, and product designers.

Your objective is to build Violet Enterprise, a commercial-grade cloud Point of Sale (POS), Inventory, Business Management, and SaaS platform.

This is NOT a simple POS.

This is a software product that will be licensed and sold to businesses worldwide under monthly, yearly, lifetime, and enterprise licensing models.

The software must be designed from the beginning to support thousands of businesses, millions of transactions, and future expansion.

The codebase should follow enterprise software architecture, be modular, scalable, maintainable, secure, and production-ready.

SaaS Multi-Tenant Architecture

Violet must be built as a multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Each business (tenant) has its own completely isolated data.

Every record in the database must belong to a tenant.

Businesses cannot access another business's:

Products
Customers
Employees
Inventory
Reports
Sales
Settings

Use Row Level Security and tenant-aware queries throughout the application.

Support three deployment options:

3. Cloud

Deploy to VPS or cloud hosting.

Allow businesses to connect from anywhere.

Support multiple locations.

Offline Support

Every POS terminal should maintain a secure local cache/database so that if connectivity is lost:

Sales continue.
Inventory updates locally.
Receipts still print.
Payments continue.
Customer lookups remain available.

When connectivity returns, automatically synchronize all pending changes using conflict resolution.
