# 26. IMPORTANT FINANCIAL DEFINITIONS

Use consistent calculations throughout the application.

### Gross Sales

Total sales before discounts and refunds.

### Net Sales

Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds

### Cost of Goods Sold

The cost associated with products sold.

### Gross Profit

Net Sales - Cost of Goods Sold

### Gross Margin

Gross Profit / Net Sales × 100

Make sure these calculations are consistent across all reports.

Do not calculate profit using the current product price if the historical transaction stored a different cost.

Historical transactions should use the product cost that was applicable when the sale occurred.
