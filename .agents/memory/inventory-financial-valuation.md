---
name: Inventory financial valuation
description: Rules for interpreting current stock value, projected profit, received inventory cost, and supplier commitments.
---

Projected gross profit and margin must be treated as incomplete whenever any product with on-hand stock is missing its cost. Do not count a missing cost as zero and present the resulting difference as profit.

**Why:** A zero fallback understates inventory cost and overstates projected profit, turning incomplete catalog data into a misleading financial claim.

**How to apply:** Cost value may still show the known subtotal with a warning. Retail value remains current stock multiplied by current sale price. Profit and margin stay unavailable until all on-hand products have costs.

Received inventory cost measures received quantity multiplied by the received unit cost. Open purchase-order totals measure commitments. Neither represents actual supplier cash paid because Violet does not track supplier invoices or payments yet.
